Ace of Swords

Ace of Swords

In shortA moment of real clarity. Say it plainly.

Upright

breakthroughclaritytrutha decisive ideacutting through

This is the moment something becomes clear enough to say out loud — an insight that rearranges everything, a decision that suddenly looks obvious, the right words finally found. The card is double-edged on purpose: this clarity will cost you something, and it is worth it.

Reversed

confusiona truth misusedclouded thinkingclarity that wounds

The clarity is missing or badly aimed. Muddled thinking, a truth used to wound rather than to sort something out, or an insight you keep almost having. It can also mean information that is technically accurate and completely misleading.

In the pictureA hand from a cloud grips an upright sword crowned with a laurel and an olive branch — victory and peace, both. Six yods fall. The mountains below are jagged and bare: the territory of pure intellect has no shelter.

The turn

Say the true thing plainly. Precision now saves months of ambiguity.
