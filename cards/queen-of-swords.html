Queen of Swords

In shortSays the true thing, kindly, without softening it.

Upright

clear judgementhonestyindependenceboundariesexperienced wisdom

She has been through it and she is not sentimental about it. This is clear-eyed perception — she sees the situation exactly, says so kindly but without padding, and keeps very good boundaries. As advice: be honest, be specific, and stop protecting people from accurate information.

Reversed

coldnessbitternessharsh wordscynicism from old wounds

Clarity has hardened into cynicism. Bitterness worn as realism, criticism that lands harder than intended, or a defence against being hurt again that has become the whole personality. It can describe someone using honesty as a weapon.

In the pictureShe sits in profile, sword upright, one hand extended — receiving or holding at bay. A butterfly on her crown, tassel at her wrist: transformation and grief both. One bird flies above; the clouds below her have cleared.

The turn

Say it accurately and kindly. Both, not one.
