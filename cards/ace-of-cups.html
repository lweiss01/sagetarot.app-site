Ace of Cups

In shortYour heart opens. Let it.

Upright

new feelingemotional openinglove offeredoverflowgrace

Something new is starting emotionally — love offered, a friendship beginning, kindness arriving that you did not earn, or a wave of feeling that surprises you. This is a gift rather than an achievement, and the only job is being open enough to accept it. Creative and spiritual openings live here too.

Reversed

guarded heartblocked feelingemotion withheldan offer declined

The cup is turned down, or nothing is flowing. This is emotion held back, an offer you cannot bring yourself to accept, or a heart that closed for good reasons and has stayed closed out of habit. It can mark grief that has not been allowed to move.

In the pictureA hand offers a cup overflowing with five streams — more than the vessel can hold. A dove descends with the host; the lotus-covered water below receives everything. The W on the cup, inverted, is the M of water.

The turn

Say yes to the offer. Receiving is the harder half of this card.
