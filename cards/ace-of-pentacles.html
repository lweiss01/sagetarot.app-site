Ace of Pentacles

In shortA solid opportunity. Take it and start.

Upright

tangible opportunitya new resourceprosperity beginninga seed

Something practical is being offered — a job, an income, a home, a resource, a genuinely useful opportunity. Unlike the other beginnings in the deck this one has weight and you can actually hold it. It needs planting rather than admiring: nothing happens while it sits in your hand.

Reversed

missed opportunitypoor timinga shaky foundationdelayed material gain

The opportunity slips, or it was never as solid as it looked. Bad timing, a foundation that will not take the weight, or a chance not taken and now gone. It can also mean money or security delayed rather than denied.

In the pictureA hand offers a single large pentacle above a walled garden in full bloom, with an arched gateway leading out to mountains. The gate is open. The pentacle floats — it has not been planted yet.

The turn

Plant it this week. Open the account, sign the thing, book the first hour.
