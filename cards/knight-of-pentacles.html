Knight of Pentacles

In shortSlow, steady, boring — and it works.

Upright

persistencereliabilitymethodical progressdoing it properlyroutine

This is the least glamorous and most dependable figure in the deck: routine kept, commitments honoured, progress made at a rate that looks like nothing and adds up to everything. Where he turns up, boring consistency is the winning strategy.

Reversed

stagnationstubbornnesstediumprogress too slow to matter

Thoroughness has become inertia. Work that has stopped moving, stubbornness about a method that no longer fits, or a routine so entrenched it has become the point. The card asks whether this is patience or just being stuck.

In the pictureHis horse is a heavy black plough-horse, standing completely still — the only stationary knight. Ploughed fields behind. He holds the pentacle up and looks at it steadily. Oak leaves on his helm and the horse's brow.

The turn

Same time, same thing, every day. Do not renegotiate it with yourself.
