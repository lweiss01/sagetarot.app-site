Ace of Wands

Ace of Wands

In shortA spark of an idea. Act on it now.

Upright

sparkinspirationnew ventureraw potentialdesire

Something has just caught your interest and there is real heat behind it — an idea, an invitation, an urge to make something. This is potential rather than achievement, and it does not keep. The energy is at its highest right now and it will not stay at this level, so use it while it is burning.

Reversed

false startdelayed launchflat energyan idea without follow-through

The spark did not catch. This is enthusiasm that fizzled out, a project you announced and never started, or a delay that took the heat out of something. It can also mean the urge is still there but you are sitting on it, waiting for a better moment that keeps not arriving.

In the pictureA hand emerges from a cloud holding a living branch, leaves still sprouting from it. Below, a castle on a hill — the potential destination, not yet approached. Eight loose leaves fall, the yods of divine spark.

The turn

Act on it within forty-eight hours or admit you will not.
