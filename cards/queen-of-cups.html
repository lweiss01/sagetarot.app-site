Queen of Cups

Queen of Cups

In shortFeels everything, and stays steady anyway.

Upright

emotional depthempathyintuitionholding otherscompassionate wisdom

This is the deepest emotional intelligence in the deck — the person others bring their grief to, who can hold it without drowning in it. She treats gut feeling as real information. As advice: pay careful attention to feelings, yours and other people's, and let them inform the decision.

Reversed

overwhelmabsorbing others' feelingsmartyrdomemotional withdrawal

The line between her feelings and everyone else's has dissolved. This is emotional overwhelm, looking after people until it becomes your whole identity, or the opposite — a withdrawal so complete that nothing gets in. Both are what happens when depth is not protected.

In the pictureHer cup is the only closed, ornate one in the deck — a lidded reliquary with angel handles. She gazes into it, absorbed. She sits at the water's edge but on stone, feet dry: in it and not in it.

The turn

Feel it, then ask what is yours to carry and what is not.
