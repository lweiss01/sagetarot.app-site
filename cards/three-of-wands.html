Three of Wands

In shortIt is underway and out of your hands. Wait well.

Upright

expansion underwayforesightships coming inwaiting wellenterprise

You have set something in motion and it is now out of your hands for a while — applications sent, work circulating, ships at sea. This is a confident wait rather than an anxious one, and the card suggests thinking bigger than you have been. What is possible here is larger than you have been planning for.

Reversed

delaysplans returning emptyshort-sightednessimpatience

What you sent out is coming back late or empty. Timelines slip, plans return with nothing, or you find you aimed too small for the effort involved. It can also mean impatience eating away at a wait that is actually going perfectly normally.

In the pictureHe stands with his back to us on a high cliff, watching ships on the water. He holds one of the three staves and the other two stand behind him — invested, not idle. The gold ground suggests the venture is already prospering.

The turn

Widen the ask. You are currently planning for less than is available.
